Our Marketing Approach

Casid Pharmaceuticals follows a structured, doctor-centric marketing model built on trust, quality, and ethical practice.

Doctor Detailing Programme

Our Medical Representatives visit registered MBBS, MD, and specialist doctors regularly, providing scientific literature, product samples and clinical data to support evidence-based prescribing.

Hospital & Institution Tie-ups

We partner with government hospitals, private nursing homes and multi-specialty clinics to ensure our formulations are available on formulary lists and prescribed by in-house specialists.

Retail Pharmacy Network

Through our distributor network, Casid products are stocked in over thousands of retail pharmacies across Tamil Nadu ensuring easy patient access and high brand visibility at the point of sale.

CME & Medical Conferences

We actively sponsor Continuing Medical Education events, product launches, and medical seminars to enhance brand credibility and engage with key opinion leaders (KOLs) in the healthcare sector.

Efficient Supply Chain

Our logistics and supply chain team ensures on-time delivery, optimal inventory management, and uninterrupted product supply to every distributor and stockist point across the state.

Pharmacovigilance & Quality

We follow stringent post-market surveillance and pharmacovigilance practices to monitor product safety, handle adverse event reporting, and maintain compliance with all regulatory standards.

Our Ethical Marketing Commitments

Casid Pharmaceuticals upholds the highest standards of responsible and ethical promotion across all marketing activities.

Science-Based Claims Only

Every marketing message we carry is backed by clinical evidence, peer-reviewed data, and approved product monographs — no exaggerated or misleading claims.

Fair Trade Practices

We adhere to all UCPMP (Uniform Code of Pharmaceutical Marketing Practices) guidelines set by the Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers, Government of India.

Patient Safety First

Our pharmacovigilance cell actively monitors product safety signals. Any adverse drug reactions are reported, investigated, and addressed promptly.

Sustainable Marketing

We are committed to reducing paper-based promotional materials and transitioning to digital detailing tools that are environmentally responsible.
