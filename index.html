About Us


CASID is a branded formulations player in the pharmaceutical formulations in India.

Since from 2000, Casid Pharmaceuticals Pvt Ltd is a pharmaceutical manufacturing and marketing company headquarted in Trichy, india. Driven by value based business operations, the company's products are known for consistent quality and ethical promotion. we have an GMP ISO certified manufacturing unit with exceptional Quality Control and our products are par with global standards.
